# 🎧 Spotify: Product Analysis & Experiment Design

## 🛤️ User Journey: From First Use to Power User

### First Touch (Year 1)
- I first used Spotify because friends shared playlists with me.
- I created a free account and instantly liked the UI — clean, responsive, and intuitive.
- Loved the ability to explore genres and moods.
- Used it mainly for discovering music, often in background while working or commuting.

### Becoming a Paid User (Year 2)
- Switched to Premium to avoid ads and download playlists.
- Started creating and sharing playlists with friends.
- Became heavily dependent on Spotify for workouts, focus sessions, and travel.

### Today (Year 4+)
- Use Spotify daily across multiple devices (phone, desktop, car).
- I rely on algorithmic features like Discover Weekly and Daily Mixes.
- Spotify Wrapped has created strong emotional connection with the product.
- The app feels personalized — I feel “known” by Spotify.

## 🧪 3 Experiments to Improve Spotify Experience

---

### 📊 **Experiment 1: Daily Mood-Based Playlists**

#### Hypothesis:
If users are offered playlists tied to their current mood (e.g. "chill", "pumped", "anxious"), engagement will increase.

#### Test Conditions:

| Cell | Condition |
|------|-----------|
| A (Control) | Current homepage layout |
| B | Homepage includes a new "How are you feeling today?" mood survey → tailored playlist |
| C | No survey — show 3 mood-based playlists based on inferred listening patterns |

#### Allocation:
- A: 50%
- B: 25%
- C: 25%

#### Metrics:
- **Leading**: Playlist starts per session, clicks on mood playlists, skip rate
- **Lagging**: Session duration, weekly retention, NPS

---

### 📊 **Experiment 2: Social Listening Sessions**

#### Hypothesis:
Allowing friends to listen in real time together will increase multi-session engagement.

#### Test Conditions:

| Cell | Condition |
|------|-----------|
| A (Control) | No social listening feature |
| B | Enable “Listen Together” button for Premium users only |
| C | “Listen Together” for all users (free + premium) with push notifications when friends go live |

#### Allocation:
- A: 40%
- B: 30%
- C: 30%

#### Metrics:
- **Leading**: Feature activation rate, number of shared sessions
- **Lagging**: Social invitations, Premium trial conversion, DAU/WAU

---

### 📊 **Experiment 3: Personalized Podcast Feed Cleanup**

#### Hypothesis:
Reducing irrelevant podcasts in “Your Shows” tab increases podcast consumption and reduces churn.

#### Test Conditions:

| Cell | Condition |
|------|-----------|
| A (Control) | Current podcast tab with all follows |
| B | Auto-hide shows not listened to in 30+ days |
| C | Prompt user weekly to clean up “Your Shows” tab

#### Allocation:
- A: 33%
- B: 33%
- C: 33%

#### Metrics:
- **Leading**: Number of podcast episode starts, cleanup actions
- **Lagging**: Monthly podcast hours per user, subscription retention

---

## ✅ Summary

- Spotify excels at personalization and emotional connection through music.
- Experiments aim to enhance that by adding **mood context**, **shared experience**, and **cleaner content surfaces**.
- Metrics were selected to balance short-term usage and long-term retention.
